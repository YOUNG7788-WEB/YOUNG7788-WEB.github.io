After Dark: a five-city video experiment

2026-11-14 · 2 min read

A bilingual night-city video and a complete publishing package.

On this page ＋
Start reading

I made a night video set in five cities and went through several versions of the footage, bilingual subtitles, cover, title and platform descriptions. When it was ready, I chose to press Upload myself and got the publishing materials ready ahead of time. I enjoyed working on the pictures, but subtitles, sourcing, rights checks, covers and file versions all matter if I want to get a film out the door.

Later I had the computer delete the final video, source clips, caches and backups. There isn't a hidden download link. This page keeps a record of how I made it. I chose to keep Parallel Universe Press, which was a separate project.

Between a video idea and a file ready to upload there are plenty of steps to miss. The shot order needs to tell the story, the subtitles need to be readable, and the cover and description should match what's in the video. With bilingual subtitles it's easy for one language to look natural while the other reads like machine translation. I had to watch the footage repeatedly with the subtitles in place.

The cleanup was my choice, without a dramatic story about giving up on the project. I needed disk space for the next thing. One process note helps me remember what I did better than ten backups with confusing names.
